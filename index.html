ABOUT ME

A creative with a technical side.

I'm Jeff Omondi Onyango, a graphic designer, motion designer, content creator and digital professional based in Kenya.

I enjoy turning ideas into visual content that is simple, engaging and easy to understand. My work covers graphic design, motion design, content creation, video editing and digital marketing.

I also have a background in software development, which gives me an understanding of websites and digital technology beyond design.

I have worked directly with customers and businesses, creating promotional materials, managing digital content and helping brands present themselves better online.
